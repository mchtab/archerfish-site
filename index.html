Studio

Archerfish Holdings builds applied AI software, drawing on a decade of work on AI products and the tooling designers and engineers use to shape them.

Most AI work stops at the demo. The interesting part starts after it: the retries, the edge cases, the moment a model has to act on someone's behalf and be trusted with the result. That is the work the studio takes on.

We take a small number of engagements a year and ship each one end to end.

Details

What we build

Agent systems

Assistants that carry out real errands end to end: placing calls, handling correspondence, running scheduled work. Each ships with the guardrails and hand-back paths that make it safe to leave running.

Interfaces for models

Tooling that gives designers and operators direct, legible control over model behaviour, instead of a prompt box and a shrug.

Evaluation and prototypes

Short builds that answer one question about a product idea, with the measurement in place to tell whether the answer held.
